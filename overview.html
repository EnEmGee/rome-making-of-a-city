Overview of the course

About this course

The short course is designed to take you about 8 hours in total. Each module should take about an hour to explore and each ends with a multiple-choice quiz where you can test your learning.

Take your time. This course is for you to enjoy. It is self-paced and there are many opportunities to explore areas of your interest with further reading options.

There is no assessment in this short course but we have designed a multiple-choice quiz at the end of each module to help consolidate your learning. The correct answers are revealed at the end of each question. You can re-take the quiz as many times as you like in order to develop your knowledge and consolidate your learning.

What you will learn

In this course, you’ll explore:

  • How key social and cultural forces have shaped the city of Rome
  • How major political events in the city of Rome’s history have shaped the city
  • The story of key individuals who have impacted the history of the city
  • How the environment out of which Rome grew has shaped life in the city
  • How to interpret original artefacts from Rome’s past as written texts, artworks and urban sites
  • How to discover more about the history of the city yourself

To complete the course:

  1. Begin with the Map of Rome.
  2. Work through Modules 1–8 in sequence.
  3. Complete all activities in sequence.
  4. Complete each module’s quiz as a way to consolidate your learning.
  5. Review the course summary.
  6. Explore the Further Reading Options to follow up your interests.

  • A PDF reader.
